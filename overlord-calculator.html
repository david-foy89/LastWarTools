Overlord Calculator FAQ

  1. What does this calculator include? Training (Joint Attack, Defense Support, Survival Training), bond rating, promotion shards/unlocks, and skill badge totals.
  2. What resources does Overlord training use? Guidebooks and Certificates for training levels, plus Bond Badges for bond rating upgrades.
  3. What does Overlord promotion cost? Promotion spends Overlord Shards and can unlock ranks and skills as you raise the level toward 60.
