Hero EXP FAQ

  1. How much EXP to max one hero? Level 1 → 175 costs about 15.45 billion EXP. A full 5-hero squad needs about 77.23 billion.
  2. What HQ do higher levels need? Each step lists an HQ gate — for example Lv 72 needs HQ 15, Lv 100 needs HQ 20, and Lv 175 needs HQ 35.
  3. One carry or the whole squad? Compare 1-hero vs multi-hero totals (up to 15) here before spending books so you do not overinvest in a single hero.
