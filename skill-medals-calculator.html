Skill Medals FAQ

  1. How many medals to max UR? 1 → 30 costs 222,000 medals. Full 1 → 40 (with Exclusive Weapon) costs 572,000.
  2. SSR / SR vs UR? SSR is about 90% of UR cost (199.8K to 30). SR is about 80% (177.6K to 30). Both cap at 30.
  3. Promoting to UR? Skill medals spent before a UR promotion are refunded — hold heavy SSR investment if promotion is soon.
