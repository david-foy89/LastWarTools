Exclusive Weapon FAQ

  1. How many shards to unlock? Unlock costs 50 named shards on a 5★ hero. Later levels can use named or universal exclusive weapon shards.
  2. Lv 20 vs Lv 30? 1 → 20 costs 1,080 upgrade shards (1,130 with unlock). 1 → 30 costs 2,830 (2,880 with unlock). The 20 → 30 push alone is 1,750.
  3. What should I target first? Push your main carry to Lv 20 before splitting shards — it is the competitive baseline and the awakening prerequisite.
